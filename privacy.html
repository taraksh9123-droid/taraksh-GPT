Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

This policy explains how Taraksh GPT, a browser-based chat, coding, and search assistant created by Taraksh Choudhary, accesses and handles information when you use the app.

Information collected and how it is used

You can use the app as a guest or sign in with Clerk. Clerk provides an account identifier and profile details made available by your account settings, such as your name, email address, and profile picture. The app uses these details to identify your account, show account information, and associate saved chats with your account.

For signed-in accounts, the app stores the Clerk account identifier and the chats you choose to save. Chat records include their titles, messages, timestamps, and any generated image attached to a chat. Clerk handles authentication credentials and sessions.

Chats, prompts, and connected services

Guest chats and app preferences are stored in your browser's local storage. When you sign in, chats are stored in the app's Cloudflare D1 database so they can be loaded across devices. When you sign in while guest chats are saved, the app copies those chats into your account. Signing out does not delete saved account chats.

  • When you use Smart chat, code review, or AI-generated chat titles, relevant prompts and conversation text are sent through the app's Cloudflare Worker to Groq to produce a response or title.
  • When you request an image, the image prompt is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI. The generated image is returned to the browser and may be saved with its chat.
  • When you use web search, your search query is sent to DuckDuckGo.
  • For weather, a city name is sent to Open-Meteo for geocoding, or (if you choose to use your current location) your browser-provided coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo for the forecast.
  • Image understanding runs in your browser using models downloaded from jsDelivr and Hugging Face. The selected image is processed locally by the browser feature and is not uploaded to the app's Worker for image understanding.

These services receive the information needed for the feature you request and process it under their own privacy policies. Clerk profile details are used only for sign-in and the account features described here. They are not sold, used for advertising or AI-model training, or sent to Groq or image-generation services by the app.

Storage, security, and retention

Clerk manages the browser sign-in session and token renewal. The app sends Clerk session tokens to its Cloudflare Worker over HTTPS. The Worker verifies token signatures against Clerk's public signing keys and does not store those tokens in the app database.

Guest conversations remain in your browser until you delete them or clear the site's storage. Signed-in chats remain in the account until you delete them. Signing out ends the Clerk session. To request deletion of account profile data, contact us using the details below. We do not set an app analytics or advertising cookie.

Optional browser features

Voice input, speech playback, and current-location weather are optional browser features. They require your browser's relevant permissions or support. Voice recognition may be processed by your browser or its provider under their own terms; the app does not send microphone audio to its own Worker. You can deny microphone or location access and continue using other features.

Your choices and contact

You can delete chats in the app, clear guest chats and preferences by clearing this site's browser storage, or sign out of Clerk. For a request to delete account profile data or for privacy questions, contact Taraksh9123@gmail.com. Include the email associated with the account if relevant, but never send your password, access token, or an authentication code.

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The current version will remain available at this page.
